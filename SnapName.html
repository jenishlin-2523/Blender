SnapName App

A mobile app to rename and search images efficiently — all in one simple, real-time app. Organize your photo library effortlessly and find any image instantly with smart, real-time search.

  • Rename images individually or in bulk for better organization
  • Add tags and metadata for faster and smarter searching
  • Access and manage your photo library quickly, all in one place
